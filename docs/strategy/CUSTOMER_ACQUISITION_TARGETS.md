specialty trades; site work, environmental and engineering), merged and deduplicated. Contact routes
are copied exactly as each firm publishes them. No email address, owner name, size, client or revenue
is guessed.

"Size" is evidence only where a number is published; otherwise it says *not published*. No firm on
this list has a verified employee count except Wolverine Low Voltage (six staff named).

returned 403, so most bid evidence is the firm's own bid/estimating language and named public
clients rather than award documents.
