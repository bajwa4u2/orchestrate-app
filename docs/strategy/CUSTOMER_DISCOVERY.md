# Orchestrate customer discovery

Opened 2026-09-16. This file records **real conversations with real buyers, and nothing else.**

There are **zero recorded discovery conversations to date.** That is the honest starting state. The
acquisition motion so far is outbound email only — Batch 1 (5 firms, sent 2026-09-15) and Batch 2
(4 firms, sent 2026-09-16), with no replies yet and no calls held. Every belief we hold about what a
contractor will pay for is currently a belief.

## Rules

1. **One entry per real conversation.** No composite entries, no imagined buyers, no illustrative
   examples. If it did not happen with a named person at a named firm, it does not go in this file.
2. **Their words, not ours.** Record the language the buyer used, verbatim where possible. The point
   of this file is to find out what they call the problem, which is almost never what we call it.
3. **Record refusals and silence too.** A firm that said no, and why, is worth as much as one that
   said yes. Most of what we need to learn is in the refusals.
4. **No stage is inferred.** A conversation is CONTACTED, HELD, or NO REPLY. Nothing moves because
   it felt positive.
5. **Nothing here is traction.** This file is evidence about a market, not a pipeline, and it must
   never be cited as customer numbers, pilots or demand.

## What each conversation should establish

- **What breaks in their week** — the specific failure, not the category.
- **What it costs when it breaks** — time, rework, a late invoice, a lost job. Their estimate.
- **What they already use** — the actual tools, including paper, WhatsApp, a whiteboard, a assistant.
- **What they already pay for** — software they currently buy, and roughly what for.
- **What they would pay to solve** — asked directly, and recorded even when the answer is "nothing".
- **Their language** — how they describe the problem in their own words.
- **What stops them buying** — price, switching cost, trust, timing, the crew won't use it.
