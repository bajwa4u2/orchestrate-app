## What changed

- Write automatically (the owner's standing yes, within a daily limit).
- Today shows a prospect's reply with the answer to send, change or leave.
- Each business is put as a proposal; follow-ups automatic; reply follow-through.
- How often to follow up, pace and the reply choice now act; the writer reads the kind's answers,
  the proof allowed in notes and the owner's rules; required wording is printed in the footer.
- A newer server needs no release: unknown Market decisions are shown as decided, payment
  choices and kind questions come from the playbook, every draft is shown before approval.
- Your plan is the last step of Setup.
