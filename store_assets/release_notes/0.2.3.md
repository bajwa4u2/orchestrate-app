# Release notes — Orchestrate 0.2.3 (13)

What testers and reviewers are told this version changed. Kept in the
repository so the words that go into a console are the same words that were
reviewed, rather than typed fresh into a text box at submission time.

## What the audience actually has

Google Play closed testing (Alpha) is on **10 (0.2.1)**, released 23 June 2026
to 7 testers. Production is inactive. So these notes span June to now — 0.2.2
and the first 0.2.3 build were never released to the track — and not just the
most recent day of work.

TestFlight is on **0.2.1 (10)**, the same June build.

## Google Play — "What's new" (500 character limit)

```
The workspace is rebuilt around relationships.

- Today shows only what needs a decision. A quiet day looks quiet.
- Signing in lands you in your workspace, never at a checkout.
- Screens no longer flash or slide over each other as you move.
- Change your own name, and see saved details everywhere.
- Press Enter to sign in.
- Plainer language, and commercial terms stated honestly.
- Tell us something, and check your version, from the account menu.
```

## TestFlight — "What to Test" (4000 character limit)

```
This is the first build since June, and the workspace has been rebuilt around
relationships rather than around lists of records.

Worth exercising:

- Sign in. You should land on Today, not on an older home screen, and you
  should never be sent to a checkout on the way in.
- Move between Today, Market, Relationships and Business. Screens should swap
  without animating over one another, and returning to one you have already
  opened should not blank behind a spinner.
- Open a relationship. It leads with why it stands where it does, not with a
  label.
- Account menu (your name, bottom left). The whole row opens it. It carries
  People & authority, Plan & billing, Account & security, "Tell us something",
  and the version you are running.
- Edit profile. You can now change your own name, which was previously fixed
  at whatever was typed when the account was created. Saving updates it
  everywhere, without signing out and in again.
- Press Enter in the sign-in form. It used to do nothing.

Known and deliberate: Orchestrate is not selling subscriptions in this build.
Commercial terms are being set with first customers directly, so where a
checkout would have been, the product now says so and offers a conversation
instead. Nothing in the app takes a payment.

Also deliberate: Orchestrate will not send anything on your behalf until a
sending mailbox of yours is connected and authorised. Business > Mailbox and
sending explains what is missing.
```

## What these notes deliberately do not say

- No feature is claimed that the build does not have.
- Nothing implies a subscription can be bought. It cannot: commercial
  activation is closed by founder decision, on every rail.
- No count, metric or customer number appears anywhere.
