What testers and reviewers are told this version changed. Kept in the
repository so the words that go into a console are the same words that were
reviewed, rather than typed fresh into a text box at submission time.

## TestFlight — "What to Test" (4000 character limit)

This is the first build since June, and the workspace has been rebuilt around
relationships rather than around lists of records.

Worth exercising:

## The rest of the App Store Connect estate, brought to 0.2.3

Release notes were the smallest part of what was stale. The listing had been
carrying metadata from earlier versions, and in two places it described an app
that no longer exists. What was changed, and why:

| Field | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Description | "managed commercial execution platform… infrastructure readiness… ICP" | The public-site voice: governed outbound under the business's own verified sending identity, with WHAT ORCHESTRATE WILL NOT DO stated plainly (1,812 chars) |
| Screenshots | 4, from an older build, captions using "ICP" | 6 at 1242×2688, rendered from this build: Today, Relationships, Market, Business, Mailbox, Business identity |
| Promotional Text | empty | "Governed outbound under your own sending identity…" |
| Subtitle | empty | "Governed outbound for business" (30/30) |
| Support URL | `orchestrateops.com` (the marketing home) | `orchestrateops.com/support` |
| App Review Notes | answers to the rejection that preceded 0.2.1 | this build, and where to look in it |
| App Privacy | Name, Email Address | Name, Email Address, User ID, Other User Content |
| Age rating | 17+ globally (16+ in most regions) | 4+ |

### App Privacy had drifted from the binary

`ios/Runner/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` — which ships inside the IPA and which
Apple reads — declares four collected types. App Store Connect declared two.
The manifest is the harder statement to argue with, so the label was brought
up to it rather than the other way round: User ID and Other User Content
added, both **used for App Functionality, linked to the user's identity, not
used for tracking**, which is what the manifest says. Nothing about the app
changed; the disclosure caught up with it.

### The age rating was 17+ for a capability the app does not have

`Unrestricted Web Access` was answered YES. Apple defines it as users being
able to "navigate to any webpage within the app or freely browse the web" —
an in-app browser. There is no webview in this repository: no
`webview_flutter`, no `flutter_inappwebview`, and every one of the eight
`launchUrl` calls uses `LaunchMode.externalApplication` or `platformDefault`,
which hands the URL to Safari and leaves the app. Corrected to NO.

`Social Media` was unanswered — it is a new question, and Apple required an
answer by 7 September 2026. Answered NO: there is no feed, no redistribution,
no discovery surface. `Social Media Disabled for Users Under 13` answered NO
as well, because the app does not call the Declared Age Range API and claiming
otherwise would be a false statement about a technical control.

Everything else in the questionnaire was already answered and was left alone.
The recalculated rating is **4+**, which is what a B2B workspace with no UGC,
no chat, no ads and no browser should be.

## Google Play and the Microsoft Store, brought to the same build

Both were further out of date than App Store Connect, and both were showing
customers a product that no longer exists.

### Google Play

| Field | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short description | "Signal-driven outreach and lead intelligence for modern teams" | "Governed outbound for your business, under your own verified sending identity" (77/80) |
| Full description | "signal-driven outreach and lead intelligence platform… continuously evolving with advanced signal intelligence" | the same 1,812-char text the App Store carries |
| Phone screenshots | 4 marketing panels | 6 renders of this build at 1080×1920, Today first |
| Data safety | 2 data types, both declared ephemeral | Name, Email address, User IDs, Photos, Files and docs |

**The screenshots were advertising a closed offer.** They were not product
screens at all — they were onboarding panels reading "Choose your service
path", "Start with outreach", "Add revenue follow-through", and a toggle for a
**"15-day start period"**. Commercial activation is closed on every rail, so
that is a purchase path visible on a store listing with nothing behind it.

**Data safety was wrong twice over.** Only Name and Email address were
declared, and both were answered *"this collected data is processed
ephemerally"* — which is why the listing summarised itself as "1 data type
collected or shared". Ephemeral means held in memory for the length of one
request; a person's name and email are stored for the life of the account, and
this session added `POST /auth/me` precisely so a name could be edited later.
Corrected to not-ephemeral, and three real collections added:

- **User IDs** — parity with the shipped `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`.
- **Photos** — `client_branding_screen.dart` picks an image for the business logo.
- **Files and docs** — `client_relationships_screen.dart` imports a contact CSV.

All five: collected, not shared, app functionality (User IDs also account
management), nothing for advertising or analytics.

Mailbox content is deliberately **not** declared. Google defines "collected"
as *transmitted off the user's device*; a connected mailbox is read
server-to-server and its content never transits the device. Apple's form asks
a different question, which is why the two labels are not identical.

Screenshots are 1080×1920 rather than the App Store's 1242×2688: the Play
Console asks for 16:9 or 9:16, and 9:19.5 is outside it.

seeded data and shown an empty Today would reasonably file it as a defect.

respond.

address) and the age rating (3+ / Everyone worldwide) were checked and are
correct. Nothing there needed changing.

**Nothing is owed on this rail.** The Credentials grid on Additional Testing

why submission 7 certified without it. The note's "credential provided with
this submission" is accurate. Do not read the empty grid as a missing
credential.
