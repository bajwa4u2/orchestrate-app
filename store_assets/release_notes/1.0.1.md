# Orchestrate 1.0.1 (15)

Corrections found by using the product on a phone and on Windows, not by
running its tests.

- Subscribing now offers both monthly and annual. The previous model could hold
  only one product per store, so annual was not merely unsold — it could not be
  represented at all.
- The pricing page states what Orchestrate costs: free entry, and one
  subscription billed monthly or annually. Both give an organisation the whole
  product; the only difference is how often you are billed.
- Fixed text on the pricing card that rendered invisible against the dark
  background on a phone.
- The support programmes are named correctly. Google for Startups and AWS
  Activate are shown as programme names rather than logos, which is the
  representation both companies permit without an issued mark.
- Removed package language — "lane", "tier", a 15-day trial — that described
  packages Orchestrate no longer sells and never did sell to anyone.
- Plan and billing no longer prints the same sentence twice.
