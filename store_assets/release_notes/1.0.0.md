### Google Play production is gated, and not by us

This is a personal developer account, so Play requires a closed test with **12
opted-in testers running for 14 continuous days** before production access can
even be applied for. The console currently reads **8 testers opted-in**, and
`Apply for production` is disabled.

So the Play half of 1.0.0 reaches closed testing now and production no sooner
than 14 days after a 12th tester opts in. No artifact or metadata change moves
that date.

No stale "beta", "preview" or 0.2.x language may survive into the listings.

## The narrative

Orchestrate is a commercial workspace. It finds businesses worth approaching,
keeps the record of every approach and reply, and refuses to send anything it
cannot stand behind.

1.0.0 is the version where that is true on every client rather than most of
them, and where the product stops describing itself as an experiment.

## TestFlight — "What to Test" (4000 character limit)

```
Orchestrate 1.0.0 (14). This is the first complete public release.

Please exercise:

1. Signing in, and signing back in after signing out. You should land in your
   workspace, never at a checkout.

2. Today. It shows only what needs a decision from you. A quiet day should
   look quiet rather than empty-but-busy.

3. Market. It should tell you how much of your market it has actually
   searched. If an area could not be searched it says so — that is a coverage
   gap being reported honestly, not a failure.

4. Relationships. Open one and check that what was sent and what came back
   read as a single conversation.

5. Business. Check that the name on correspondence and the name on agreements
   are both correct, and that it tells you truthfully whether the business can
   send at all.

6. Navigation. Every screen should have a way back, and the system Back
   gesture should do what you expect from anywhere.

7. Account. Your own details, your devices, and account deletion. Deletion is
   real and cannot be undone — please do not use it on an account you want.

Known limits in this build are listed for App Review in the review notes.
```

## What must not be claimed

- No platform's behaviour may be described from another platform's evidence.
- Purchase and subscription behaviour is not described as certified until it
  has been exercised on the store's own sandbox rails.
- No production customer, agreement, payment or reply is fabricated to
  populate reviewer state. Any demo state is identified as demo state.
