Investor overview

The commercial relationship is continuous. The software running it is not.

Orchestrate runs B2B relationships from market opportunity through agreement, delivery, and payment, in one governed operating environment.

CRM records the relationship. Orchestrate operates it.

The problem

One line of work. Half a dozen systems that do not know about each other.

A business's commercial life runs as a single line: who the customer is, what was agreed, what was delivered, what was accepted, what was invoiced, what was paid. Market intelligence, CRM, email, contracts, project tools, invoicing, payments, approvals and now AI tools each own a fragment. None owns the thread.

Every boundary between those systems is a place where the record restarts, and where something can be sent, promised or billed that nobody sanctioned.

Nineteen years running this exact line, by hand.

What businesses lose

Fragmentation is not an inconvenience. It costs five specific things.

Context

The person delivering the work cannot see what was promised to win it.

Authority

Nobody can say who was allowed to commit the business to this, or when that was decided.

Continuity

The relationship survives. The record of it restarts at every tool boundary.

Explanation

"Why did we pursue them, and what happened?" has no answer that survives staff turnover.

Consequence control

Every handoff is a place where something can be sent, promised or billed that nobody sanctioned.

Why now it matters

All of that was survivable while software only recorded work. It stops being survivable when software starts performing it.

The Orchestrate model

The relationship is the durable unit of account.

The unit is the relationship rather than the deal or the transaction. Orchestrate begins with the market, before contact exists, and it does not end when the invoice is paid.

Business intent Market Relationship Engagement

Across every step: Attention · Authority · Reasoning · Provenance · Recovery · AI as a governed instrument

Not every counterparty becomes a relationship

The market is possibility. A relationship exists once an admitted fact establishes durable commercial context with an identifiable counterparty. It never begins because a score crossed a line.

Payment does not end it

An engagement is one undertaking inside a relationship. When it closes, the relationship remains, with its history, its context and its authority intact for the next one.

How it works

Four territories, and one question each.

Today

What needs me, and what is moving

A projection across everything else. Attention means human work is genuinely owed, not a notification count.

Market

Who may be worth pursuing, and why

Comparative judgment across many counterparties, with the reasoning and the counter-evidence both available.

Relationships

Who we deal with, and what is happening

Continuity with one counterparty: condition, the one meaningful blocker, the current engagement, the next consequential act.

Business

How Orchestrate should operate for us

Configuration and governance, including who may act in the company's name, and how far.

Market and Relationships are separate because they are different work shapes, not different data. One is judgment across many; the other is depth with one.

Governed AI

AI creates the leverage. It does not acquire the authority.

AI observes, reasons, drafts, classifies and prepares work across the whole line. A deterministic check stands in front of anything that leaves a mark on the outside world.

What AI does here

Understands the market, assembles the evidence behind a judgment, drafts communication from admitted facts, summarises what came back, prepares the next action, and says what is missing.

What it is never permitted to do

Decide that a business may commit, send, agree or bill. Authority here is explicit, granular and revocable. Communication authority is not contractual authority, and contractual authority is not financial authority. Being able to take an action is a different grant from being able to delegate it.

The commercial value here is governance in service of speed. A business can operate at machine pace and still answer, months later, who authorized an action, on what evidence, and how to undo it.

Who needs it first

A narrow entry point into a broad market.

Initial wedge

Owner-led commercial contractors and specialist service firms

Roughly 5–50 people. They win work through proposals or tenders, deliver in stages, invoice against work their customers accept, and keep the same counterparties across many jobs. Michigan first.

Platform market

Any business whose work runs engagement to payment

The wedge is a go-to-market decision, not a definition. Orchestrate is not contractor software; contractors are simply where the fragmentation is sharpest and the buyer is reachable.

The properties that concentrate the pain: staged delivery against agreed scope, long-lived counterparties, expensive or reputational errors, an already-fragmented stack, and an owner personally in the loop.

Business model

Priced primarily at the organization level.

Platform subscription

Recurring, at the organization level, for the maintained governed environment: durable commercial context, relationships, authority, provenance, continuity, security and core capability, with a sensible included operating capacity.

Operational consumption

Expansion where material usage creates real marginal cost or real operating value: AI processing, communication and provider services, execution volume.

Implementationoptional

Charged only where genuine assisted work is required: migration, configuration, integration or deployment support. Not every customer needs it, and self-service customers are not charged for work nobody performed.

Pricing scales primarily with the breadth and intensity of commercial operation Orchestrate carries, with usage-sensitive expansion where material consumption creates real cost or value.

The reasoning behind that is commercial rather than conventional. A purely seat-driven model gives a buyer a reason to keep part of the business outside the system, and an incomplete commercial record is the thing this product exists to prevent. So the primary axis is the work Orchestrate carries, not the number of people who touch it.

Price points are being set from unit economics and willingness-to-pay evidence with the first customers.

These are good products with real customers, and a business can assemble much of this lifecycle from them today. Several will overlap with Orchestrate on individual features.

Orchestrate's difference is structural, not featural. Existing systems organize primarily around a stage, a record, a module or a workflow, so the commercial relationship is handed repeatedly across those boundaries. Orchestrate's bet is that the relationship itself remains the durable unit of account across the complete commercial line, which is what lets authority, reasoning and provenance belong to every step rather than to one of them.

Why now

AI can now participate in commercial work. The controls for letting it have not caught up.

Software can increasingly perform consequential commercial work, not only record it. What businesses still lack is the continuity, authority and consequence control that would let them hand that work over across a whole relationship.

The leverage arrived first

Drafting, classification, reasoning over commercial context and preparation of the next action are all now practical inside ordinary business workflows.

The cost of ungoverned action rose with it

The same capability that sends the right message can send a wrong one, commit to the wrong terms, or bill for work nobody accepted, at speed and at volume.
