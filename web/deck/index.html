Questions owners ask

Before you start

Will it send anything I have not seen?

No. Every first note and every answer waits for your yes. A standing yes is your choice, with a daily limit, and you can switch it off.

Where do the businesses come from?

Public sources, such as open business directories and each business's own website, plus public records where Orchestrate watches them for your kind of business. Each card says where the business was found and why it fits.

Does it read my email?

Only the conversations with the businesses it is working on for you. It never reads or comments on anything else in your inbox.

What if I already use Jobber or a CRM?

Keep them. Orchestrate works before the job exists: finding the customer and carrying them to an agreement and payment.

How long does setup take?

Eight short steps about your business and who you want to work with. It is free, and you see where you stand before you choose a plan.
